# EFIS System Design — Garmin Blue Room

## Session Overview
Garmin's advanced system design session covering the architecture and integration of their EFIS product line including G500 TXi, G600 TXi, and G3X Touch displays. Aimed at experienced avionics technicians working on complex panel installations.

## Key Takeaways
- EFIS system architecture and data bus topology for Garmin displays
- Proper sensor selection and placement (ADAHRS, ADC, magnetometer)
- Integration with navigators, autopilots, and transponders via ARINC 429 / RS-232
- System design considerations for single vs. dual screen installations
- Power distribution and cooling requirements for display systems

## Products Covered
- Garmin G500 TXi / G600 TXi
- Garmin G3X Touch
- Garmin GI 275
- Garmin G5
