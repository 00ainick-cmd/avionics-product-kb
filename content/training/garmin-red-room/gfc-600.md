# GFC 600 — Garmin Red Room

## Session Overview
Advanced installation and configuration training for the Garmin GFC 600 high-performance digital autopilot. Covers the differences from the GFC 500, airframe-specific considerations for more complex aircraft, and advanced features like yaw damping and electric trim.

## Key Takeaways
- GFC 600 vs. GFC 500 — capability and airframe differences
- Installation considerations for Bonanzas, Barons, and other high-performance aircraft
- Yaw damper and electric trim integration
- Advanced autopilot modes (coupled approaches, VNAV, flight director)
- GI 275 as the primary attitude source for GFC 600
- Flight test procedures specific to the GFC 600

- Garmin GFC 600
- Garmin GI 275
