# EWIS Master Training — DMC

## Session Overview
Comprehensive Electrical Wiring Interconnection System (EWIS) training covering inspection, maintenance, and repair of aircraft wiring systems. Essential for technicians working on aging aircraft where wiring integrity is a critical safety concern.

## Key Takeaways
- EWIS regulatory requirements and inspection standards
- Wire degradation modes: chafing, heat damage, fluid contamination, aging
- Visual and instrumented inspection techniques
- Repair procedures for damaged wiring
- Proper harness routing, clamping, and protection
- Documentation requirements for EWIS maintenance
