The top 3 posts that stay at the top of your profile

whether they will follow or leave.

WHAT ABOUT HASHTAGS?
Hashtag Phase-Out
Historically, hashtags helped platforms
categorize and discover content.
They were the main way posts appeared in topic
searches.
What’s Changed
AI now reads images, captions, and on-screen
text to understand posts.
Platforms summarize what your content is
about automatically.
What Matters More Now
Clear captions with relevant keywords
Text on images and graphics
Content that clearly communicates the topic
