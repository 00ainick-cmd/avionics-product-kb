# SMS Coordinator Training — AEA

## Session Overview
Comprehensive Safety Management System (SMS) coordinator training. Covers the four pillars of SMS, implementation requirements for Part 145 repair stations, and practical tools for building a safety culture in avionics shops.

## Key Takeaways
- Four pillars of SMS: policy, risk management, assurance, promotion
- SMS requirements for Part 145 repair stations
- Hazard identification and risk assessment processes
- Building a safety reporting culture
- Practical SMS documentation and record-keeping
- Coordinator responsibilities and day-to-day management
