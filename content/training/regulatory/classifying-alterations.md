# Back to Basics: Classifying Alterations — AEA

## Session Overview
Foundational regulatory training on how to properly classify avionics installations as major or minor alterations under FAA regulations. Essential knowledge for every avionics technician handling field approvals and STCs.

## Key Takeaways
- Major vs. minor alteration classification criteria (14 CFR Part 43)
- When an STC is required vs. when a field approval (Form 337) is appropriate
- Documentation requirements for each classification
- Common classification mistakes and their consequences
- Working with your local FSDO on borderline cases
