# Avionics Wire Prep Master Class — DMC

## Session Overview
Hands-on master class from DMC on proper avionics wire preparation techniques. Covers stripping, crimping, soldering, lacing, and connector assembly using industry best practices and proper tooling.

## Key Takeaways
- Proper wire stripping techniques for avionics-grade wire
- Crimping standards and tool selection (open barrel, closed barrel, D-sub)
- Soldering best practices for avionics connections
- Wire lacing patterns and techniques (LaceLok and traditional)
- Connector assembly and pin insertion procedures
- Quality inspection criteria for completed wire assemblies
