# International Regulatory Update — AEA

## Session Overview
International regulatory update covering EASA, TCCA, and other non-FAA regulatory developments affecting avionics installations on internationally registered aircraft.

## Key Takeaways
- EASA regulatory updates affecting GA avionics
- TCCA (Transport Canada) policy changes
- International validation of FAA STCs
- Cross-border maintenance and compliance considerations
- Import/export requirements for avionics equipment
