# FlyGarmin Administration — Garmin Blue Room

## Session Overview
Training on the FlyGarmin dealer administration portal — managing database subscriptions, software updates, STC documentation, and customer account management for Garmin avionics products.

## Key Takeaways
- Navigating the FlyGarmin dealer portal
- Managing database and chart subscriptions for customers
- Processing software updates and service bulletins
- STC documentation and compliance tracking
- Customer account setup and management best practices

## Products Covered
- Garmin GTN 750Xi / GTN 650Xi
- Garmin GI 275
- All Garmin panel-mount products requiring database updates
