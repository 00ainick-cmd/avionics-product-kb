# Troubleshooting CMS & IFE Networks — ALTO Aviation

## Session Overview
Troubleshooting training for cabin management systems (CMS) and in-flight entertainment (IFE) network architectures. Covers network topology, common failure modes, and systematic diagnostic approaches for ALTO and similar cabin systems.

## Key Takeaways
- CMS and IFE network architecture and topology
- Common network failure modes and symptoms
- Systematic troubleshooting methodology
- Network diagnostic tools and techniques
- Cable testing and connector inspection procedures
- Integration issues with aircraft power and grounding
